Redwoods, mountains, ocean, and gardens

Muir Woods Redwoods

All day FREE entrance 1–2 hr walk

Towering old-growth coast redwoods up to 258 ft tall. FREE entrance on Memorial Day (US citizens/residents). Parking $10 or shuttle $4 — book in advance!

gomuirwoods.com

1000 Muir Woods Rd, Mill Valley

Mount Tamalpais — Matt Davis to Stinson

5–6 hours FREE ~7.5 mi, +1,800 ft

Full-day workout: mountain golden hills, redwood forests, waterfalls, all the way to Stinson Beach on the Pacific. One of the best hikes in the Bay Area.

awanderingturtle.com

Pantoll Ranger Station

Crissy Field — Picnic + Golden Gate Views

All day FREE

Flat waterfront promenade with BBQ grills and iconic views of the Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, and the SF skyline. Bring a picnic!

presidio.gov
